# Common Questions
Q: What is Epsilon3?
A: Epsilon3 is a procedure execution platform that enables teams to execute, track, and manage complex operational workflows in real time.

Q: What does “procedure execution” mean?
A: Procedure execution means turning static instructions into live, guided, and trackable workflows that ensure tasks are performed correctly and consistently.

Q: How is Epsilon3 different from a checklist or SOP tool?
A: Epsilon3 enforces real-time execution, tracks every step, validates inputs, and provides full auditability, rather than simply storing procedures.

Q: Who is Epsilon3 for?
A: Epsilon3 is designed for teams operating in high-complexity, safety-critical environments such as aerospace, defense, manufacturing, and energy.

Q: What problems does Epsilon3 solve?
A: Epsilon3 solves the gap between planned work and actual execution by ensuring procedures are followed correctly, progress is visible, and outcomes are traceable.

Q: How does Epsilon3 fit with ERP, MES, or PLM systems?
A: Epsilon3 complements these systems by acting as the execution layer where work is performed, while ERP/MES/PLM handle planning, tracking, and design.

Q: How does Epsilon3 improve reliability?
A: Epsilon3 improves reliability by guiding operators step-by-step, enforcing correct sequencing, validating actions, and capturing execution data.

Q: How does Epsilon3 support compliance and audits?
A: Epsilon3 creates step-level audit trails with timestamps, operator attribution, and version-controlled procedures, enabling fast and defensible audits.

Q: Can Epsilon3 scale across teams and locations?
A: Yes. Epsilon3 supports distributed teams and ensures consistent procedure execution across facilities and organizations.

Q: How does Epsilon3 help with scaling operations?
A: Epsilon3 enables scaling by standardizing execution, reducing variability, capturing institutional knowledge, and providing real-time visibility.

Q: What is the execution layer?
A: The execution layer is where planned work is carried out. Epsilon3 ensures that work is performed correctly, not just recorded after completion.
