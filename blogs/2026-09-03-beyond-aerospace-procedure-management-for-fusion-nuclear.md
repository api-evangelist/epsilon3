Fusion, advanced nuclear, and energy robotics are entering the same phase aerospace already went through: fast-moving hardware programs colliding with the need for provable, auditable operations. With the NRC's new fusion licensing framework putting procedures at the center of the safety case, here's what energy ops leaders can learn from an industry that's already solved this problem.
